# Remote Work Policy

Kestrel Outfitters, People team. In effect from January 1, 2026.

## Who can work remotely

Employees in headquarters roles can work remotely up to 3 days a week. Store and warehouse staff work on site, because their work can't be done from home.

## Core hours

Remote employees are available from 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. in their own time zone, and keep their calendar up to date so teammates know when they can meet.

## Home office stipend

Employees who can work remotely get a one-time stipend of $500 for a desk, a chair, or a monitor, and $40 a month toward their home internet.

## Working from another country

With their manager's approval, employees can work from another country for up to 4 weeks a year. Longer stays need approval from the People team, because of tax and visa rules.
