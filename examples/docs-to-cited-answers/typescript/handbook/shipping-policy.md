# Shipping Policy

Kestrel Outfitters, Customer Support. In effect from January 1, 2026.

## Processing time

Orders placed before 1 p.m. Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later ship the next business day.

## Shipping options

Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days and costs $5.95. It's free on orders over $75.

Expedited shipping takes 2 business days and costs $14.95.

Overnight shipping arrives the next business day and costs $29.95. It isn't available for PO boxes.

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states and to Canada. Orders to Canada take 5 to 10 business days, and duties are paid at checkout. We don't ship to other countries yet.

## Lost or late packages

If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, customers can contact Customer Support for a replacement or a refund.
