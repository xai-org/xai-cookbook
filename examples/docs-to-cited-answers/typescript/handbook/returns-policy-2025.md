# Returns Policy (2025)

Kestrel Outfitters, Customer Support. In effect from January 1, 2025, to December 31, 2025.

## Standard returns

Customers can return unworn, unwashed items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items need their original tags attached.

## Sale items

Items bought on sale can be returned within 45 days of delivery. Sale returns are refunded as store credit, not to the original payment method.

## Final sale items

Gift cards, personalized items, and items marked "Final sale" on the product page can't be returned or exchanged.

## Return shipping

Every order comes with a prepaid return label, so return shipping is free for all customers.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the return arrives at our warehouse. Store credit is emailed the same day the return is processed.

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged or develops a defect, follow the Warranty Policy instead of returning it.
