# Returns Policy (2026)

Kestrel Outfitters, Customer Support. In effect from January 1, 2026. Replaces the 2025 Returns Policy.

## Standard returns

Customers can return unworn, unwashed items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items need their original tags attached.

## Sale items

Items bought on sale can be returned within 14 days of delivery, for a refund to the original payment method. After 14 days, sale items are final sale and can't be returned or exchanged.

## Final sale items

Gift cards, personalized items, and items marked "Final sale" on the product page can't be returned or exchanged.

## Return shipping

Return shipping is free for Kestrel Club members. For everyone else, a $6.95 return shipping fee is taken out of the refund.

## Refunds

Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the return arrives at our warehouse.

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged or develops a defect, follow the Warranty Policy instead of returning it.
