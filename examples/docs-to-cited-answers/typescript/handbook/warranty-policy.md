# Warranty Policy

Kestrel Outfitters, Customer Support. In effect from January 1, 2026.

## The Kestrel Lifetime Guarantee

We repair or replace any Kestrel-brand product that fails because of a defect in materials or workmanship, for as long as the customer owns it.

## What isn't covered

The guarantee doesn't cover normal wear and tear, damage from accidents or misuse, or products altered by anyone other than Kestrel. Products from other brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty.

## Making a claim

Customers start a claim from their order page, or by emailing warranty@kestrel.example with a photo of the problem and proof of purchase.

## Repairs and replacements

Repairs take 2 to 3 weeks, and we pay for shipping both ways. If a product can't be repaired, we replace it with the same item. If it's no longer made, the customer gets store credit for the price they paid.
