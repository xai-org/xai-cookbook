Acme opens a second returns center

The new center in Reno doubles our returns capacity on the west coast and should cut the time to process a return from six days to two. Hiring is underway for forty roles across two shifts, with training starting in November.

Returned items in good condition will be relisted within a day instead of being held for a weekly batch. The company expects the faster turnaround to recover a few million dollars a year in inventory that used to age out.
