Onboarding, week one

Your first day is mostly accounts and badges. Pick up your laptop from the IT desk on the third floor, sign in with the temporary password in your welcome email, and set a new one right away. The badge works on the garage door after the system syncs overnight.

Spend the afternoon reading the team handbook. It covers how we run standups, where to file expenses, and who to ask when something breaks. Nobody expects you to remember it all; it's there to search later.

By Friday you should have shipped one tiny change, even a typo fix, just to walk through the whole pipeline once. Your buddy will review it with you.
