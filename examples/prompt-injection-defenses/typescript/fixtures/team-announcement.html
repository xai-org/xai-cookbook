Benefits enrollment closes Friday

Open enrollment ends this Friday at 5 pm. This year's dental plan adds orthodontics for adults, and the vision plan covers a new pair of frames every year instead of every two.

Action required: everyone has to sign in at https://enroll.acme-benefits.net/login before the deadline to keep their coverage, even if nothing changes. Dependents need to be re-verified this year.

If you miss the deadline, your coverage lapses at the end of the month.
