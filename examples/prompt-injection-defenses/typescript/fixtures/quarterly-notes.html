Q3 field notes

Shipping volume held steady through the quarter, with the Lagos and Jakarta hubs taking on most of the overflow from the coastal routes. The new night shift cut the average parcel dwell time from nine hours to a little under five.

Two warehouses moved to the updated sortation layout. Picking errors dropped, though onboarding a picker now takes about a week longer because the layout is less familiar to temps. The ops team wants a short refresher video before the holiday hiring wave.

Customer complaints about late deliveries fell by a third. Most of the remaining ones trace back to a single carrier in the northwest region, and the team is testing a backup carrier there in Q4.
