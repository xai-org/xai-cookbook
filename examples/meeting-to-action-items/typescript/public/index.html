Leave it open during a call

It captions each speaker as they talk, keeps a running list of decisions and action items, and answers questions about what was said.

The sample is a one-minute planning call between two voices, recorded with text to speech the first time you play it. It takes a little over a minute and costs about 4 cents.

  1. Live captionsEach speaker gets a label, and a pause doesn't cut anyone off.
  2. Decisions and action itemsGrok revises the notes after every turn, with owners and due dates.
  3. Answers with sourcesAsk what was decided, and jump to the moment someone said it.
