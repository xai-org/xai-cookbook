# Copperleaf Kitchen refund policy

- Refunds are available for 30 days after delivery.
- Damaged or defective on arrival: we refund the full price of each affected item. The customer doesn't need to send it back.
- Changed your mind: unused items in their original packaging can be returned for the item's price minus a $7.50 return shipping fee. We email a prepaid return label and refund right away.
- Shipping is refunded only when every item in the order arrived damaged.
- Final sale items, such as gift cards, can't be refunded.
- Appliances come with a one-year warranty. After the 30 days, a broken appliance can be repaired or replaced under warranty, but not refunded.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method and show up in 5 to 10 business days.
- A refund can never be more than what's left of the order total after earlier refunds.
