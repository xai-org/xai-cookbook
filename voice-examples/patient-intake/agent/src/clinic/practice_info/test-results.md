How lab and imaging results are released to patients.
The front desk cannot read, interpret, or release any test result. Results are released only by the ordering provider or their nurse, because a number without context can be misleading.

Most lab results post to the patient portal within three business days. Imaging takes up to five business days. The practice contacts the patient directly for any result that needs action, whether or not the patient calls first.

A patient who wants results discussed can have a message sent to the provider's nurse for a call back, or book a follow_up or telehealth visit to go through them.

Portal login help is handled by the front desk. A reset link is sent to the email address on file.
