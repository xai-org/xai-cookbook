What to bring, what to do before an appointment, and arrival instructions.
Every patient should bring a photo ID, a current insurance card, and a list of every medication they take, including over the counter medicines, vitamins, and supplements.

New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to finish registration. Returning patients should arrive 5 minutes early.

For an annual physical, patients should fast for 8 hours beforehand if labs are expected, though water and regular medications are fine. For a visit about an injury, patients should bring any imaging or paperwork from urgent care or the emergency department.

A patient under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, or must bring a signed consent form from one.

Patients who need records from another practice should ask that practice to fax them to 555-0143 at least three business days before the visit.
