How refill requests are handled and how long they take.
The front desk cannot approve, deny, or send a refill. Only the prescribing provider can, and every request is reviewed against the chart.

The fastest route is for the patient to ask their pharmacy to send an electronic refill request to the practice. Patients may also leave a refill request with the front desk, which goes to the provider's nurse.

Routine refills are processed within two business days. Requests are not processed on weekends or holidays. Controlled substances always require an office visit and are never refilled over the phone.

A patient who has run out of a medication and cannot wait should say so, and the message is marked urgent for the nurse.

Refills are only issued for patients who have been seen within the last twelve months.
