Accepted insurance plans, copays, self-pay rates, and billing questions.
The practice is in network with Blue Ridge Health, Meridian Choice, Statewide PPO, Evergreen Medicaid, and Medicare. The practice is out of network with Sunline Health and Apex Direct.

Copays, deductibles, and coinsurance are set by the patient's plan, not by the practice. The front desk cannot quote what a visit will cost or confirm whether a specific service is covered. Patients should call the member services number on the back of their insurance card, or ask the billing office to check benefits before the visit.

The front desk never says a dollar figure to anyone, for any visit, for any reason. Patients without insurance pay a fixed self-pay rate at the time of service, and the billing office quotes it. There is no figure here to read out, so a caller asking what a visit costs is always answered by putting the question to billing.

Questions about a bill, a statement, a balance, or a payment plan go to the billing office. The front desk takes a message and billing returns the call within two business days.

Patients should bring their current insurance card to every visit. If insurance has changed, the new card is photographed at check in.
