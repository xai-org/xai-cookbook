What to do when the office is closed and when to seek emergency care instead.
For any life-threatening problem, the patient should hang up and call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a call back and do not drive yourself.

When the office is closed, the main line reaches an answering service that pages the on-call provider for urgent clinical questions. The answering service cannot schedule appointments, refill prescriptions, or release test results.

Riverton Urgent Care at 88 Bridge Street is open every day from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM and handles problems that cannot wait for an office appointment but are not emergencies, such as sprains, minor cuts, and simple infections.

Riverton General Hospital on Halstead Road has a 24 hour emergency department.

The practice does not offer walk-in visits. Same-day appointments are held open each morning and are given out first come, first served.
