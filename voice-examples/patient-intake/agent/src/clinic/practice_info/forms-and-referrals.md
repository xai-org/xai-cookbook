Referrals to specialists, medical records requests, and paperwork the office completes.
Referrals are ordered by the provider, not by the front desk. Most plans in network here do not require a referral to see a specialist, but the patient's plan decides that. A referral request goes to the provider as a message and is answered within three business days.

Medical records requests need a signed release and take up to ten business days. Records sent to another treating provider are free. Records sent to the patient or a third party may carry a copying fee.

School, camp, sports, work, and disability forms are completed by the provider within five business days. Forms are dropped off at the front desk or uploaded to the portal. Some forms need a recent visit before they can be signed.

Prior authorizations for medications and imaging are handled by the practice's clinical staff and typically take five to ten business days depending on the plan.
