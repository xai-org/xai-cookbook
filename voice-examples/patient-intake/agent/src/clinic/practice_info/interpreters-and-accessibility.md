Interpreter services, accessibility, and other accommodations for a visit.
Interpreter services are free to the patient and are arranged by the practice, never by the patient's family. A phone interpreter is available in more than two hundred languages at any visit with no notice. An in-person or video interpreter, including American Sign Language, needs three business days' notice.

The building is fully accessible: a ramp at the Maplewood Avenue entrance, an elevator to the second floor, accessible exam rooms, and a wheelchair available at the front desk on request.

Service animals are welcome. Pets are not.

Patients who need a longer appointment, a quieter waiting area, or help completing forms should say so when booking, and the note is added to the appointment.

Written materials can be provided in large print or in the patient's preferred language on request.
