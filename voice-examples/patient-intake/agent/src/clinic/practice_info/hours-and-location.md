Office hours, address, parking, and how to reach the practice.
Maplewood Family Medicine is at 412 Maplewood Avenue, Suite 200, in Riverton.

Office hours are Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM, and Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The office is closed Saturday and Sunday, and closed for lunch from 12:00 to 1:00 every weekday. The last appointment of the day starts 30 minutes before closing.

Appointments are only offered during office hours. There are no evening, weekend, or holiday appointments.

The office is on the second floor. There is an elevator in the main lobby. Parking is free in the lot behind the building, and there is street parking on Maplewood Avenue. The number 14 bus stops directly outside.

The main phone line is 555-0142. The nurse line and the billing office share that number and are reached through the menu.
