Who the practice may speak to about a patient and what identity checks are required.
Before anything from a chart is discussed, the caller must give the patient's last name and full date of birth. Nothing about a chart, an appointment, a medication, or a result is confirmed or denied until that matches.

Chart information is released only to the patient, to the parent or legal guardian of a patient under 18, or to someone the patient has named in writing on a release form. A spouse, an adult child, or a friend is not automatically authorized. Without a release on file the practice cannot confirm that a person is even a patient here.

Anyone may book, move, or cancel an appointment on a patient's behalf once the patient's last name and date of birth are verified. That is scheduling, not chart information.

A release form is available at the front desk and on the portal, and takes one business day to process.

At 18 a patient's chart becomes confidential from their parents unless the patient signs a release.
