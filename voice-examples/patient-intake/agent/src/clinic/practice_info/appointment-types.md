The kinds of visits the practice offers and how long each one takes.
The practice offers five kinds of visit:

- sick_visit: a same-day or next-day visit for a new problem such as a sore throat, a rash, an earache, or a fever. 20 minutes.
- annual_physical: a yearly preventive exam for an adult, including screening and lab orders. 40 minutes. Whether a plan covers it, and what it leaves the patient to pay, is decided by the plan and never by the front desk.
- well_child: a routine check for a patient under 18, including growth, development, and immunizations. 30 minutes.
- follow_up: a review of an ongoing condition or a recheck after a previous visit or a hospital stay. 20 minutes.
- telehealth: a video visit for a problem that does not need an exam, such as a medication check or reviewing results. 20 minutes. Telehealth is not appropriate for a new problem that needs to be looked at, listened to, or touched.

Patients under 18 are seen by Doctor Priya Raman. Adults may see any provider. New patients are welcome with any provider currently accepting new patients.

Patients should arrive 15 minutes before a first visit and 5 minutes before a return visit.
